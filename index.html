Biology & Cellular Respiration — Study Notes

1. Glycolysis (Cytoplasm): Glucose (6-carbon) is split into two molecules of pyruvate (3-carbon), yielding a net gain of 2 ATP and 2 NADH molecules without requiring oxygen.

2. Citric Acid Cycle (Mitochondrial Matrix): Pyruvate is oxidized into Acetyl-CoA, releasing CO₂ and generating NADH, FADH₂, and 2 ATP per glucose molecule.

3. Oxidative Phosphorylation (Inner Mitochondrial Membrane): Electrons travel down the electron transport chain, creating a proton gradient that drives ATP synthase to produce ~28–32 ATP.
